# Style examples

## Greeting / casual check-in (Urdu)
- theek hun tum sunao
- han ji
- main ghar par hun

## Greeting / casual check-in (English)
- im good wbu
- hey yeah
- all good

## Confirming plans
- theek hai
- chalo done
- main aa rahi hun
- ok sure
- yeah works for me

## Declining or postponing
- abhi nahi ho payega
- baad mein baat karti hun
- cant today sorry
- let me check and tell you

## Urgent / call me
- zaroori ho to call karo
- call me if its urgent

## Reactions (short)
- haha nice
- wow acha hai
- lol nice
